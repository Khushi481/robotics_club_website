Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries about the club.

How can I join the Robotics Club?

Recruitments are held annually for freshers. Follow our social media pages for updates on induction sessions. No prior experience is required!

Do I need to know coding?

Not necessarily. We have different domains including Mechanical Design, Electronics, and Management. However, a basic interest in technology helps!

Can I collaborate on a project?

Yes! We welcome collaborations with other technical clubs and external organizations. Reach out to us via the contact form above.

Where is the lab located?

Our lab is located in the Student Activity Center (SAC), Room 102, inside the NIT Patna campus.
