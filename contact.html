FAQ

How can I join the Robotics Club?

Recruitments are usually held at the beginning of the academic session. Keep an eye on our social media handles for announcements regarding inductions/auditions.

Do I need prior experience in robotics?

Not necessarily! We welcome enthusiasts of all levels. While experience is a plus, passion and willingness to learn are what we value most. We conduct workshops to help you get started.

Where does the club meet?

Our official club room is located in the Student Activity Center (SAC). We usually meet there for projects, discussions, and workshops.
