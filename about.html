ABOUT US

Introduction To Best
Robotics Club!

Founded with a vision to revolutionize the technical landscape at NIT Patna, the Robotics Club has grown from a small group of enthusiasts into a powerhouse of innovation and engineering excellence.

We provide a collaborative environment where students can explore the boundaries of robotics, automation, and artificial intelligence, turning theoretical knowledge into real-world impact.

Our Mission

To foster a culture of technical innovation and empower students through hands-on robotics education and competitive challenges.

Our Vision

To become a leading hub for robotics research and development, producing the next generation of global technology leaders.

Club Objectives

Achieving excellence in national competitions, conducting impactful workshops, and developing cutting-edge autonomous systems.

Club History

Established in 2015, the Robotics Club of NIT Patna was formed by a passionate group of students who wanted to bridge the gap between classroom learning and practical robotics applications. Over the years, we have transitioned from simple line-followers to advanced autonomous rovers and drones.

Our journey has been marked by numerous accolades in national events like Inter-IIT Tech Meet, Technex, and various robotics championships across India.
